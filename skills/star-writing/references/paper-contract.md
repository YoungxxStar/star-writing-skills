# Paper Contract

This bundled file is an immutable template. With explicit authorization, copy
it to `.star-writing/paper-contract.md` relative to the resolved project root
and use that copy as the living project-specific record. Keep the working copy
short enough to refresh before consequential edits. Do not treat its contents
as evidence unless they point to the responsible source.
