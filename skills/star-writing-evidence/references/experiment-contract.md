# Experiment and Analysis Contract

Use this template before running a decisive study or when reconstructing the
meaning of an existing result. Adapt the fields to computational, experimental,
observational, theoretical, qualitative, or mixed-methods work.

## Identity and Status

```text
Study or analysis ID:
Research stage:
Question:
Claim tested:
Hypothesis:
Status: planned / exploratory / development / confirmatory / completed
Responsible artifacts:
```

## Interpretation Boundary

```text
Observation expected if the claim is true:
Strongest rival explanation:

```

## Decision Timing

Record when every consequential choice was made:

| Choice | Before target outcomes | During development or validation | After final outcomes | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Do not retroactively describe a post-hoc subset, horizon, endpoint, exclusion,
aggregation, or example as prespecified.

## Completion Record

```text
Executed configuration or protocol:
Raw or minimally processed output:
Analysis script or derivation:
Displayed result:
Deviations from the planned contract:
Claims affected by deviations:
Verification performed:
```

The contract records what a result can identify. It does not force every study
into a randomized or confirmatory design.
